About

About This Project

EcoRoute is an educational prototype developed as an Environmental Studies university project. It demonstrates how a travel-planning interface can help users compare transportation choices based on distance, fuel consumption and estimated CO₂ emissions, and encourages more environmentally conscious travel decisions.
